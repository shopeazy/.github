## What you get

One account gives you a whole shop, run from a laptop or a phone.

- **Sell online.** Your store on `yourname.shopeazy.co` or your own domain: products, bookings, events and tickets, memberships.
- **Get paid.** Cards through your own iKhokha, Yoco or Peach account, plus EFT. Money goes from your gateway straight to your bank. We never hold it.
- **Deliver.** Book The Courier Guy or a PUDO locker from the order, or deliver yourself.
- **Sell in person.** The till on an iPad: Scan to pay, receipts, cashier PINs, offline mode.
- **Make it look good.** AI product photos, promo videos and listings written for you.
- **Get found.** The ShopEazy store directory, social posting, discounts, reviews and basket reminders.

Plans start at R199 a month. Try Growth free for 7 days, no card needed.
